# Animals, elements and cycles — reference

**How to read this file.** The calendar parts (the order of the animals, the years table, the Lunar New Year dates, the element cycle) are a calendar system: factual and checkable. The traits and the compatibility schemes are **tradition**, labelled as such wherever they appear. Tradition varies between sources; this file presents one common version, and the user's own tradition overrides it (see the profile).

## The 12 animals, in order

The order is fixed and never changes. Tradition explains it with the legend of a great race; the legend is a story, the order is the calendar fact.

These traits are a traditional portrait, not a personality test. Two people of the same animal share a birth-year sign, nothing more is claimed.

## The 5 elements and the 60-year cycle (calendar, simplified and documented)

On top of the 12 animals runs a cycle of **10 heavenly stems**. The stems pair up into **5 elements**, in this fixed order: **Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water** (Bois, Feu, Terre, Métal, Eau). Each element therefore governs **two consecutive years**: the first is its **yang** year, the second its **yin** year. Then the next element takes over.

Simplified rule used by this skill (documented simplification: the full stem names are not needed for year-animal work):

- Element of a year = read it in the table below; the pattern repeats every 10 years.
- Polarity alternates strictly: yang, yin, yang, yin… 2024 yang, 2025 yin, 2026 yang, and so on.
- 12 animals × 5 elements = the full combination repeats every **60 years**. The last Fire Horse year before 2026 was 1966; the next is 2086. A person's 60th year, when their exact animal + element combination returns, is traditionally marked as a major milestone (tradition).

## Traditional compatibility (tradition, labelled)

Two schemes are commonly taught. Both are **tradition**: a lens for conversation, never a verdict on real people.

### Affinity triangles

The 12 animals form 4 triangles of animals said, traditionally, to understand each other:

- **Rat, Dragon, Monkey** — traditionally the driven, inventive group.
- **Ox, Snake, Rooster** — traditionally the steady, precise group.
- **Tiger, Horse, Dog** — traditionally the free-spirited, loyal group.
- **Rabbit, Goat, Pig** — traditionally the gentle, harmonious group.

### So-called opposite pairs

Animals 6 apart in the order are traditionally called opposites or clashing pairs: **Rat/Horse, Ox/Goat, Tiger/Monkey, Rabbit/Rooster, Dragon/Dog, Snake/Pig**. Tradition reads these as friction or as complementary tension, depending on the source. This skill presents the pairing and both readings when asked, and never tells a user what it means for a real relationship.

### The rest

Pairs in neither scheme are traditionally read as neutral: workable, unremarkable, decided by the people rather than the calendar. That, too, is tradition, and it is the honest answer for most pairs.
