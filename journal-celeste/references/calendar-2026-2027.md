# Calendar 2026–2027 — Moon phases, retrogrades, eclipses

The skill's only source of dates. All dates are given as calendar dates (UTC-based published calendars, cross-checked across at least two sources at build time); an event can fall on the adjacent local date depending on the user's timezone and the exact hour — when it matters, announce the date with that caveat instead of inventing an hour. Signs for New Moons are the sign the Sun is in (the New Moon is always in the Sun's sign); Full Moon signs are the opposite sign. Near a sign boundary (cusp), a sign can be off by one — say "à un jour près" when the date is within a day of a boundary.

Extending this calendar: use published ephemeris-based calendars (Swiss Ephemeris / NASA JPL-derived sources), cross-check two sources, and note the new range here. Never fill a date from model memory.

## Retrogrades

## Seasonal markers (approximate, ±1 day)

- Equinoxes: ~Mar 20 and ~Sep 22–23. Solstices: ~Jun 21 and ~Dec 21.
- Solar return: the user's birthday — tradition treats it as a personal new year; a natural moment for an annual journal review if the user wants one.

## Traditional lenses (for alerts — always attributed to tradition)

- **New Moon** — beginnings, intentions, planting a seed of attention.
- **Full Moon** — culmination, visibility, release; many users report lighter sleep — the journal tests that for *this* user.
- **Mercury retrograde** — review, revision, re-reading; communication and tech friction are the classic themes. The modern soft reading: build in buffer, double-check.
- **Venus retrograde** — revisiting relationships, tastes and spending patterns.
- **Mars retrograde** — energy, anger and drive turned inward; pacing.
- **Eclipses** — stronger turning points in the tradition; treated as amplified New/Full Moons, never as omens.
- **Outer planets** (Jupiter → Pluto, Chiron) — slow, background themes; most users track them only if they chose the transits module.
