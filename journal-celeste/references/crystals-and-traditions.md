# Crystals and traditions

Correspondences below are **one common modern tradition**, compiled from widely published zodiac-crystal guides. Traditions vary — sometimes a lot — and no correspondence is a fact. The user's own stones and associations, recorded in their profile, always override this table. The assistant says "selon la tradition la plus répandue…" / "in the most common tradition…" when it uses this table, and never presents a crystal as healing, protective in a physical sense, or medically useful in any way.

## Practices the tradition describes (offer, never prescribe)

- **Full Moon "recharge"** — the popular ritual of placing crystals under the full Moon overnight. If the user practises it, the journal can note which stones were out and any intention attached.
- **New Moon intention stone** — choosing one stone to keep nearby for the lunation that starts.
- **Carry of the day / week** — one stone linked to the current period (e.g., a Mercury-retrograde stone for review and patience — commonly cited: Fluorite, Amazonite). Presented as tradition, picked by the user.
- **Care notes are physical, not mystical**: some stones are soft or light-sensitive (selenite dissolves in water, amethyst fades in long sun) — practical care facts may be given as such, clearly separated from any traditional meaning.

## How the skill uses this file

- At onboarding, suggest the 3 traditional stones for the user's Sun sign (and Moon sign if declared), then record **the user's actual inventory** — the inventory, not the table, is what later suggestions draw from.
- During a tracked period, a crystal suggestion is one line, optional, and always from the user's own inventory when it is known.
- In the journal, crystal notes are entries like any other ("pleine lune : quartz rose dehors, intention notée") — the review never credits or blames a stone for an outcome.
