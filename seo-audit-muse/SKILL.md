Use SEO Audit for Muse to audit <your-site>, from its own Search Console
data, not guesses.

1. State the property, the period, and the Search Console lag. Use bulk
   performance data only: clicks, impressions and positions by query and
   by page. Never copy a number from a summary without recounting it.
2. Find, in order: near-miss pages (positions 4 to 10, impressions, zero
   clicks), broken links, and crawled but unindexed pages. For each,
   state the evidence: impressions, position, clicks, and last crawl
   date when known.
3. Cross-check with on-site navigation for the same period: page views,
   on-site searches, and install or copy actions. Say plainly whether
   the bottleneck is on Google (shown, not clicked) or on the site
   (reached, not converted). Do not fix a page problem when the
   bottleneck is on Google, and do not rewrite titles when the page
   is not indexed at all.
4. Prepare fixes as separate packages, prepared only: hub titles and
   metas (title total 60 characters or less, meta 150 to 160 characters,
   benefit first, paid warning at the end when a paid API is required),
   quick fixes, near-miss metas, language redirects, and unindexed
   pages (live check first, internal links, indexing requests one by
   one in Search Console, new validation only after a new crawl).
5. For bulk title changes, generate proposals first, on the home PC
   when possible: insert « Skill » before « for Muse » or « pour Muse »,
   in French also handle the English « for Muse » pattern found in some
   French titles, check every total at 60 or less, check Muse in every
   title, hold back titles that would exceed, titles that would duplicate
   an existing Skill title, and approved prototypes, and review the lot
   mechanically before anyone applies it.
6. Stop there. Publish nothing, deploy nothing, and touch no
   production database. Deployment waits for a separate go, and
   re-measurement waits 7 to 14 days after deployment, impressions
   AND clicks, not position alone, before anything else is rewritten.
